Ideas by Jewelry Type

Character limits vary. Here is what fits on each piece.

📿 Necklace / Pendant

Typically 15–25 characters

  • A name or initials
  • A date (01.15.2019)
  • A short word: "Brave" / "Loved" / "Grace"
  • Coordinates of a meaningful place
  • A small symbol: ∞ ♡ ✦ ☽

⌚ Watch / Back of Watch

Typically 40–80 characters

  • Full name + date
  • A short quote
  • A message from the giver
  • "To [name], with love, [date]"
  • A song lyric line

💍 Ring

Typically 10–20 characters inside band

  • Initials + date: "J&M 06.14.22"
  • A single word: "Forever" / "Always"
  • "I love you" in another language
  • A personal nickname
  • Roman numerals of a date

🔖 Bracelet / Cuff

Typically 20–35 characters

  • A name + short phrase
  • "She believed she could"
  • A mantra or daily reminder
  • Birthstone month + year
  • A meaningful date in full

Engraving Tips

Get the most out of your personalized piece.

01

Keep it short

Fewer words carry more weight. "Always" hits harder than a full sentence. When in doubt, cut it in half.

02

Use a date that matters

Wedding dates, birthdays, the day you met — dates make engraving deeply personal and impossible to duplicate.

03

Check the character limit first

Ask the seller how many characters fit before finalizing your text. Too long and the engraving will be unreadable.

04

Proofread three times

Engraving is permanent. Double check spelling, dates, and punctuation before you submit your order.

05

Coordinates are underrated

GPS coordinates of where you met, got married, or grew up create a beautifully subtle, meaningful message.

06

Consider the recipient

Funny works for some people. Sentimental for others. Think about their personality, not just the occasion.
